Our Story

Fifteen years of putting people on the rides they love

Who We Are

Velocity Motors started back in 2026 with a simple idea: create a place where riders and drivers can find quality machines without the usual dealership headache. What began as a small garage with just six bikes and two cars has grown into a trusted name in the industry.

We are not your typical sales lot. Every member of our team actually rides or drives what we sell. That means when you ask questions, you get real answers from people who understand what you are looking for.
